Marquee Support

Marquee is a player for IPTV playlists you already have. It doesn't include any channels or content.

Adding a source

Open Settings (the gear on Home on iPhone and iPad, or the Settings tab on Apple TV) and choose Add Source. Paste an M3U or M3U8 link, enter an Xtream Codes server address, username and password, or (on iPhone and iPad) pick a playlist file.

A stream won't play

Marquee tries a second player automatically if the first can't open a stream. If neither works, the stream may be offline, geo-restricted or need a different User-Agent; your provider can tell you which. You can also choose a player in Settings → Player.

Artwork or details are wrong

Artwork comes from TMDB, matched on each title's name and year. Titles that don't match confidently are left without artwork rather than shown with the wrong film's.
