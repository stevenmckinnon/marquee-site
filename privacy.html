Privacy Policy

Marquee TV · Last updated 8 November 2026

Marquee doesn't collect, store or share any personal data with its developer. There are no accounts, analytics, ads or crash reporting.

What stays on your device

Playlist addresses, your library, My List and watch progress are stored only on your device. Xtream Codes passwords are kept in the device Keychain. Deleting the app removes them.

iCloud sync

If you leave Sync with iCloud on (Settings in the app), Marquee uses your own iCloud account to keep your sources, My List and watch progress the same on your devices. Source addresses and Xtream passwords travel through iCloud Keychain, which is end-to-end encrypted. The rest is stored in your iCloud key-value storage as anonymous identifiers and times. This data is held by Apple for you; the developer cannot see it. You can turn syncing off at any time.

Hidden categories and the Apple TV Top Shelf

Which categories you hide, and the PIN that protects them, stay on your device (the PIN is kept as a salted hash in the Keychain). On Apple TV, Marquee shares your Continue Watching and My List titles with its own Top Shelf extension through an app group, so they can appear on the Home Screen. That data never leaves the device, and categories you've hidden are left out of it.

Who the app talks to

  • Your playlist provider, to download your playlist and TV guide and to play streams, using the addresses and logins you enter.
  • TMDB (themoviedb.org), to fetch artwork and descriptions. The app sends the titles it's looking up. TMDB's privacy policy applies to those requests.

Children

Marquee doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
